### Section: FAQ

H2: Questions, answered

1. **Is this guide up to date?**
   Every figure was checked on {{figuresCheckedOn}} against official Government of Canada and provincial pages, and every figure is dated in the book. Where a figure could only be confirmed by reputable secondary sources, the book says so. Buyers receive updated 2026/27 editions free, starting after Canada publishes its 2027–2029 immigration levels plan.

2. **Can this guide get me a visa?**
   No guide can. Decisions are made by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada. This guide helps you choose the right route, meet its requirements and avoid the mistakes that commonly cause refusals.

3. **I only have an HND. Is there anything for me?**
   Yes. The guide explains how HND holders are usually assessed, what that means for your points, and which routes and second credentials can strengthen your profile.

4. **Do I need IELTS?**
   Every Canadian economic route to permanent residence requires an approved language test. The guide shows you which tests are accepted, the scores each route needs, and how a higher score can matter more than almost anything else.

5. **Is this legal advice?**
   No. It is general information that you apply yourself. For complex cases, such as a past refusal or misrepresentation concern, the guide explains when a licensed professional is worth paying for and how to verify one.

6. **How do I receive it?**
   Checkout is handled by Selar. You get instant access to the PDF downloads after payment, on your phone or computer.

7. **I'm not sure Canada fits me. Should I still buy it?**
   The Pathway Finder will tell you quickly. If Canada is a longer-term project for you, Part 12 shows how to build towards it and when Germany may be the better Plan B.

8. **Can I share the PDF with friends?**
   Each copy is licensed to one buyer. Please point friends to this page instead.

9. **Who is behind Japa Right?**
   Japa Right is an independent publisher of DIY relocation guides. We are not affiliated with IRCC, any government or any immigration consultant, and we do not sell visas, jobs or placements.
